4 Cold Email Templates Every Fresher Needs

Copy the subject and body, personalise the [brackets], and hit send.

Template 1: Applying Directly to HR

Subject

Application for Software Engineer | [Your Name]

Copied!
Email Body
Hi [Recruiter's Name], I hope you're doing well. I came across the opening for the [Role] at [Company] and found it closely aligned with my skills and experience. I have attached my resume for your review. I would really appreciate it if you could consider my profile for the role. Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you. Best regards, [Your Name] Phone: XXXXXXXX LinkedIn: [Link]
Copied!

Template 2: Asking an Employee for a Referral

Subject

Referral Request for [Role] at [Company]

Copied!
Email Body
Hi [Name], I hope you're doing well. I noticed you're working at [Company] and wanted to reach out regarding the [Role] opening. I believe my experience in [mention 1–2 relevant skills/projects] aligns well with the role. If you feel my profile is a good fit, I'd be grateful if you could refer me. I've attached my resume for your convenience. Thank you for your time and consideration. Best regards, [Your Name]
Copied!

Template 3: Reaching Out to a Hiring Manager

Subject

Interested in the [Role] Position

Copied!
Email Body
Hi [Hiring Manager's Name], I recently came across the [Role] opening on your team and wanted to express my interest. With experience in [mention relevant experience], I've worked on projects involving [brief achievement or technology]. I believe I can contribute meaningfully to your team. I've attached my resume and would appreciate the opportunity to discuss how I can add value. Looking forward to hearing from you. Regards, [Your Name]
Copied!

Template 4: Follow-up Email (5–7 Days Later)

Subject

Following Up – Application for [Role]
